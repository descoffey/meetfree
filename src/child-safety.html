Child Safety Standards

Last updated: August 2026

Our Commitment

MeetFree has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) of any kind. MeetFree is intended exclusively for adults, and we take active steps to keep minors off the platform and to respond swiftly to any report of suspected abuse or exploitation.

Age Requirement

Users must confirm they are 18 or older during registration. Accounts found to belong to a minor, or found to be used to target or exploit a minor, are removed immediately upon discovery.

Reporting In-App

Every user profile and conversation in MeetFree includes a built-in Report and Block feature, accessible directly from the app. Reports of suspected CSAE, underage users, or predatory behaviour are reviewed as a priority.

Our Response Process

When a report involving suspected child sexual abuse or exploitation is received, we act as follows:

1. The reported account is immediately reviewed and, where appropriate, suspended or removed pending investigation.
2. All evidence relevant to the report is preserved.
3. Where required by law, matters are reported to the relevant authorities, including the National Crime Agency (NCA) and the Internet Watch Foundation (IWF) in the UK, or equivalent bodies internationally.

Contact

To report a concern directly, or for any questions about our child safety practices and compliance, contact descoffey@gmail.com. This inbox is monitored personally and reports are treated as urgent.

External Resources

If you believe a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. In the UK, concerns can also be reported directly to the Internet Watch Foundation or the CEOP Safety Centre.
