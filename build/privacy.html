Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Who We Are

MeetFree is a plant-based dating app currently in development. This privacy policy explains how we collect and use your personal data when you sign up to our waiting list at meetfree.uk.

2. What Data We Collect

We currently collect only your email address when you register for our waiting list. We do not collect any other personal information at this stage.

3. Why We Collect It

We collect your email address solely to notify you when MeetFree launches and to keep you informed about early access opportunities. We will only contact you about MeetFree.

4. Legal Basis

We process your data on the basis of your explicit consent, given when you tick the consent checkbox on our signup form. You can withdraw this consent at any time.

5. How We Store Your Data

Your email address is stored securely by Netlify (our hosting provider). Netlify is GDPR compliant and their data processing terms can be found at netlify.com/gdpr-ccpa. We do not store your data on any other systems at this time.

6. How Long We Keep Your Data

We will keep your email address until you request deletion, or until 12 months after MeetFree launches — whichever comes first. After this point your data will be permanently deleted.

7. Who We Share Your Data With

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with any third parties for marketing purposes. Your data is only shared with Netlify for the purpose of storing form submissions.

8. Your Rights

Under GDPR you have the right to: access the data we hold about you, request correction of inaccurate data, request deletion of your data, withdraw consent at any time, and lodge a complaint with the ICO (ico.org.uk).

To exercise any of these rights, please email us at descoffey@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

9. Cookies

We do not currently use cookies or tracking technologies on meetfree.uk.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as MeetFree develops. We will notify waiting list members of any significant changes by email.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy please contact us at descoffey@gmail.com.
